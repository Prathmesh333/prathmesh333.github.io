# Generated asset prompts

The current portfolio uses one generated raster image: a late-night workbench that feels personal rather than product-like. The featured project covers are code-drawn SVG so Anime.js can animate them directly.

## Current hero: late-night workbench

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: personal portfolio hero background
Primary request: an atmospheric editorial night-workbench scene for a machine-learning engineer's personal portfolio
Scene/backdrop: jet-black desk, open graph-paper notebook with loose hand-drawn model diagrams and small illegible code-like marks, a mechanical pencil, part of a dark laptop at the edge, subtle paper scraps
Style/medium: cinematic editorial photography with a slightly tactile film-grain finish, realistic materials, not futuristic concept art
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition; desk details concentrated on the right and lower-right; generous dark negative space on the left for large website typography
Lighting/mood: low-key warm light with one papaya-orange long-exposure streak curving through the notebook, focused, curious, late-night builder energy
Color palette: jet black, graphite, warm ivory paper, brushed steel, papaya orange #ff6a00 as the only saturated color
Constraints: no people, no readable words, no logos, no brand marks, no UI mockups, no sci-fi towers, no neon cyberpunk city, no watermark
Avoid: glossy SaaS product render, dashboard imagery, green accents, blue accents, generic stock-photo polish
```

Output: `assets/generated/hero-workbench.webp`
